IngredientsMain BaseBitter leaves: $500\text{ g}$ fresh or frozen pre-washed bitter leaves (if unavailable, substitute with washed spinach or kale).   Raw skinless peanuts (groundnuts): $2\text{ cups}$ (about $400\text{ g}$).Water: As needed for boiling and blending.   ProteinsBeef or Goat meat: $500\text{ g}$ (1 lb), cut into bite-sized pieces.Smoked fish or Stockfish (Mukanjo): $200\text{ g}$ (soaked and cleaned).Fresh Shrimp/Prawns: $300\text{ g}$ (peeled and deveined).Aromatics & SeasoningsOnions: 2 large (1 for blending, 1 thinly sliced for garnish).   Garlic: 5–6 cloves.Ginger: 1 inch piece.Ground dried crayfish: $\frac{1}{2}\text{ cup}$.Vegetable oil: $\frac{3}{4}\text{ cup}$.   Bouillon cubes (Maggi/Knorr or Maggi Crevette): 2–3 cubes.Salt & Pepper: To taste.   Habanero or Scotch Bonnet pepper: 1 (optional, for mild heat).   InstructionsStep 1: Prepare the Bitter LeavesIf using raw bitter leaves: Boil the leaves in water with a pinch of baking soda or rock salt to help soften them and retain green color. Drain and wash vigorously under cold running water, squeezing out the greenish liquid multiple times until the desired bitterness level is reached.   Squeeze & Set Aside: Squeeze into tight balls to remove excess water, then loosen the leaves and set aside.Step 2: Cook the Meats & FishIn a large pot, combine the beef, half of a chopped onion, salt, black pepper, and 1 bouillon cube.Add water to cover and simmer until the meat is tender.   Add the soaked smoked fish or stockfish during the last 15 minutes of boiling.Reserve the meat stock (around 2–3 cups) in the pot with the meats.Step 3: Prepare the Peanut PasteIn a separate saucepan, boil the raw skinless peanuts in water for 10 minutes, then drain.   Transfer the boiled peanuts to a blender along with 1 large onion, garlic, ginger, and optional habanero pepper.   Blend with a little water until it forms a creamy, slightly textured paste (not completely liquid).   Step 4: Combine & Simmer the StewPour the blended peanut paste directly into the pot with the cooked meats and broth. Stir well over medium heat.   Add the ground crayfish and remaining bouillon cubes. Stir continuously for 10–15 minutes as it simmers to prevent the bottom from burning.   Add the washed bitter leaves to the pot. Stir thoroughly to incorporate the leaves into the peanut base.   Reduce the heat to low and let the stew simmer gently for another 10 minutes until thick and well combined. Adjust seasoning with salt if needed.   Step 5: Frying the Shrimp & Onion GarnishIn a skillet, heat the vegetable oil over medium-high heat.Season the fresh shrimp with a pinch of salt and pepper, then sauté in the oil for 3–4 minutes until pink.   Add the sliced onion to the skillet with the shrimp and sauté until soft and translucent.   Pour the hot oil, shrimp, and fried onions directly over the simmering pot of Ndolé as a finishing garnish. Stir gently before serving.   Traditional Serving SuggestionsServe Ndolé hot alongside any of the following staples:   Bobolo or Miondo (fermented cassava sticks)Boiled Ripe/Unripe Plantains   Boiled Yams or White Rice
